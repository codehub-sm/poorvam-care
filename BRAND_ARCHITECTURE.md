# Poorvam Brand Architecture & Multi-Business Structure

## Brand Hierarchy

```
POORVAM (Parent Brand)
├── Child Development Center
├── Hearing Center  
└── Ucube
```

## Brand Guidelines

### Color Palette
- **Primary Blue**: #2563EB (Blue 600)
- **Secondary Green**: #059669 (Emerald 600)
- **Accent Colors**: 
  - Light Blue: #DBEAFE (Blue 100)
  - Light Green: #D1FAE5 (Emerald 100)
  - Dark Blue: #1E40AF (Blue 800)
  - Dark Green: #047857 (Emerald 800)

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Serif (for headings and brand elements)
- **Secondary Font**: Sans-serif (for body text and UI)

## Business Line Definitions

### 1. Child Development Center
- **Focus**: Therapies and counselling for developmental disorders
- **Services**: Speech therapy, occupational therapy, behavioral interventions, autism support
- **Target**: Children with developmental delays, autism, ADHD, learning disabilities
- **Color Theme**: Blue primary with blue accents

### 2. Hearing Center
- **Focus**: Hearing tests, aids, and trials
- **Services**: Audiological assessments, hearing aid fitting, cochlear implants, tinnitus management
- **Target**: All age groups with hearing concerns
- **Color Theme**: Blue primary with cyan accents

### 3. Ucube
- **Focus**: Enrichment/after school skills
- **Services**: Dance, Yoga, Music, Art&Craft, Podcast and events, public-speaking, soft skills
- **Target**: Children and teens seeking skill development
- **Color Theme**: Green primary with emerald accents

## Navigation Structure

### Main Navigation
- Home
- Services (dropdown with 3 business lines)
- About
- Team
- Contact
- Book Online

### Service-Specific Pages
Each business line should have:
- Dedicated landing page
- Services overview
- Team section
- Contact form
- Pricing packages
- Onboarding process

## Implementation Strategy

### Phase 1: Brand Unification
1. Update all components to use consistent Poorvam branding
2. Implement blue/green color scheme
3. Create business line specific color variations

### Phase 2: Navigation Restructure
1. Update header navigation
2. Create service-specific routing
3. Implement business line context switching

### Phase 3: Content Restructure
1. Redesign homepage to showcase all 3 business lines
2. Create dedicated sections for each business
3. Update team, services, and contact components

### Phase 4: Enhanced Features
1. Business-specific onboarding flows
2. Service-specific pricing packages
3. Enhanced contact forms with business line context
