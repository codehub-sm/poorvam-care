Our Leader

Apoorva Rai, MASLP

Clinical Director

Our team is led by Apoorva Rai, licensed by the Rehabilitation Council of India and the Indian Speech and Hearing Association, with over 13 years of dedicated practice.

She brings extensive pediatric experience working with children with Cochlear implants, Autism Spectrum Disorder, learning disabilities, ADHD, developmental delays, and Articulation Disorders.

Her approach combines sensory-based strategies, play-based therapy, and behavioral techniques to achieve meaningful outcomes for every child.
