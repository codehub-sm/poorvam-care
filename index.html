Our Therapy Services

We offer a range of specialized therapy services designed to help your child thrive and develop essential skills.

Speech Therapy

Helping children develop clear speech, language understanding, and effective communication skills through fun, engaging activities.

Occupational Therapy

Developing fine motor skills, sensory processing, and daily living activities to help children gain independence and confidence.

Social Skills Group

Building friendship skills, emotional regulation, and social awareness in a supportive group environment with peers.

Developmental Education

Customized learning support to help children reach developmental milestones and build foundational skills for success.

Preschool Training

Preparing young children for school success with playful activities that develop pre-academic and social-emotional skills.

Remedial Education

Targeted support for children with learning differences to build academic skills and develop effective learning strategies.

Meet Our Team

Our team includes qualified, licensed, and experienced professionals dedicated to your child's success.

Apoorva Rai, MASLP

Clinical Director

Our team is led by Apoorva Rai, MASLP who is licensed by the Rehabilitation Council of India and Indian Speech and Hearing Association, and has been a therapist for over 13 years.

She has extensive pediatric experience working with children with Cochlear implants, ASD, learning disabilities, ADHD, developmental delays, and Articulation Disorders.

Her approach combines sensory-based strategies, play-based therapy, and behavioral techniques to achieve maximum results for each child.
