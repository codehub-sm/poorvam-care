# Poorvam Care — Google Maps / Google Business Profile Playbook

**Goal:** Rank #1 in the Google Maps "local pack" and Maps app for "speech therapy Electronic City," "child development centre near me," "autism therapy Bangalore," and similar queries — and become the answer AI tools give.

**Reality check (read this first):** The website is already strong. Maps ranking is decided almost entirely by your **Google Business Profile (GBP)**, not the website. Google ranks local results on three factors: **Relevance** (categories, services, description), **Distance** (proximity of the searcher — you can't change this, but you *can* win on the other two), and **Prominence** (reviews, review velocity, citations, engagement). Everything below targets Relevance and Prominence. This is work you do in the GBP dashboard (business.google.com) — I've ordered it by impact.

### 3. Set the primary category precisely
Category is the #1 relevance signal. For each listing:

Do NOT leave categories generic ("Medical clinic"). Specific categories win specific searches.

## Priority 1 — Reviews engine (the biggest prominence lever)

You're at ~61 reviews / 4.5★. Top-ranking competitors in Bangalore therapy usually have 100–300+. Reviews drive ranking through **count, average rating, velocity (how recently), and keyword content**.

### Build a repeatable review system
1. **Get your short review link** from the GBP dashboard ("Ask for reviews" → copy link). It looks like `g.page/r/...`.
2. **Ask every satisfied family, every week.** The best moment is right after a visible milestone ("she said her first word!"). Hand them a QR code at the centre and send a WhatsApp with the link the same day.
3. **Target velocity:** aim for **8–12 new reviews per month, split across both locations.** Steady beats a one-time burst (bursts look manipulative to Google).
4. **Encourage keyword-rich reviews.** Gently prompt: "If you can, mention the therapy your child received and our location." A review saying *"best speech therapy in Electronic City"* helps you rank for exactly that.
5. **Reply to 100% of reviews within 24 hours** — positive and negative. In replies, naturally include your service + location ("Thank you! We're glad our occupational therapy team at Electronic City could help Aarav."). Replies are indexable text and a ranking/engagement signal.
6. **Never buy or fake reviews.** Google's 2024–2026 review-spam detection will strip them and can suspend the listing.

### WhatsApp review-request template (copy/paste)
> Hi [Parent name], it's been wonderful working with [Child]! If you have a moment, a quick Google review really helps other parents in Electronic City find us. It takes 30 seconds: [your g.page link]. Thank you so much 💙 — Team Poorvam Care

## Priority 2 — Fully complete every GBP field
A 100%-complete profile ranks better and converts better. For both listings, fill:

- **Business description** (750 chars): lead with what + where. e.g. *"Poorvam Care is a multi-disciplinary early intervention centre in Electronic City, Bangalore offering speech therapy, occupational therapy, ABA, and special education for children with autism, speech delay and ADHD. RCI-registered, led by Apoorva Rai (MASLP), 13+ years' experience."*
- **Services**: add every service as a separate item (Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, ABA Therapy, Special Education, Parent Counselling, Therapeutic Enrichment, Autism Therapy, Hearing Assessment) — each with a 1–2 line description. These become searchable.
- **Attributes**: "Identifies as woman-owned" (if applicable), wheelchair accessible, appointment required, LGBTQ+ friendly, online care/teletherapy — all boost relevance for filtered searches.
- **Opening hours** + **special hours** for holidays (kept accurate — Google demotes listings with "hours may differ").
- **Photos**: this matters more than people think. Upload **15–20+ real photos** (exterior with signage so people recognise it, reception, therapy rooms, team, sessions in progress with consent). Add **3–5 new photos every month** — fresh photos are an active-listing signal. Add a short **video** (30–60s centre tour).
- **Products/Packages**: list your therapy packages with prices or "starting from" — GBP shows these and it's a strong conversion + relevance signal.
- **Booking link / Appointment URL**: point to https://poorvamcare.in/contact.
- **Opening date**: set your founding year (adds legitimacy/age signal).

## Priority 3 — Google Posts (weekly, forever)
GBP Posts are free real estate that signal an active, engaged business (a ranking factor) and show up in your listing.

- **Cadence:** 1–2 posts/week per location.
- **Rotate these types:**
  - *What's New*: a therapy tip, a milestone story (with consent), staff spotlight.
  - *Offer*: free screening camp, assessment discount, sibling offer — with a clear CTA + phone.
  - *Event*: summer therapy camp, parent workshops, awareness days (World Autism Day, etc.).
- Always include a keyword ("speech therapy in Electronic City") and a **Call** or **Book** button.
- Posts expire from prominence after ~7 days, which is exactly why weekly cadence matters.

---

## Priority 4 — Q&A section (seed it yourself)
The Questions & Answers block on your listing is public and indexable, and most businesses ignore it.

- **Seed 8–10 Q&As yourself** (allowed): ask the question from one account, answer from the business account. Use the real questions parents ask, mirroring your website FAQ: cost, what age to start, do you treat autism, teletherapy, how many sessions, RCI registration, parking, languages spoken.
- **Monitor daily** and answer any parent question within hours — an unanswered question (or a wrong answer from a random user) costs you leads.

## Priority 6 — Proximity workaround (you can't move, but you can widen reach)
Distance is fixed, but you can capture nearby-area searches:
- The website already has neighbourhood landing pages (HSR, Koramangala, BTM, Whitefield, Marathahalli). Keep those indexed and linked from GBP posts.

- Encourage reviews that mention *where the family travelled from* ("we come from HSR Layout") — this teaches Google you're relevant to those areas.
