Frequently Asked Questions

What age should I start speech therapy for my child?

Early intervention is most effective when started before age 3. If your child is not babbling by 12 months, not using single words by 18 months, or not combining words by age 2, consult a speech-language pathologist. At Poorvam Care, we work with children as young as 12 months to support communication development.

Do you work with children with autism?

Yes. Autism therapy is one of our core specialties. We provide speech therapy, ABA (Applied Behaviour Analysis), occupational therapy, and special education tailored for children on the autism spectrum. Our team creates individualized therapy plans based on each child's unique needs and strengths.

How many sessions does a child typically need?

The number of sessions depends on the child's condition, severity, and therapy goals. Most children attend 2–3 sessions per week. Some see noticeable improvement within 3–6 months, while others benefit from longer-term support. We conduct regular assessments and adjust the therapy plan as your child progresses.

What is the difference between speech therapy and occupational therapy?

Speech therapy focuses on communication skills — helping children speak clearly, understand language, and express themselves. Occupational therapy addresses fine motor skills, sensory processing, coordination, and daily living activities. Many children benefit from both therapies working together, which is why Poorvam Care offers integrated multi-disciplinary care under one roof.
